| **Goal** | Identify key cost drivers to inform insurance pricing strategy |

---

## 📈 Key Insights

### 1. Smoker Status — Strongest Cost Driver
Smoking is the single biggest predictor of high medical costs. On average, **smokers pay 3× more** than non-smokers, regardless of other factors.

### 2. BMI Threshold Effect
BMI shows a clear positive correlation with charges. Costs rise sharply once BMI crosses **30 (Obese threshold)** — a critical inflection point for risk segmentation.

### 3. Regional & Demographic Risk Groups
- The **Southeast region** has the highest average medical charges
- **Male smokers** represent the highest-cost risk segment in the dataset
